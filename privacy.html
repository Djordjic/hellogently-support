Privacy at HelloGently

Effective date: 25 September 2026. This information describes the native HelloGently app and support by email. The separately hosted web prototype has its own hosting-platform access and data handling.

Who is responsible?

Publisher: Miroslav Djordjic, Switzerland.
Contact: hellogently.support@proton.me.

Information on your device

The app stores your latest situation, dinner preferences, progress, preparation checklist, interest, notes and text-size preference on your device. Practice answers are temporary. The current app does not send this saved content to the publisher and has no app account, advertising SDK or app analytics service.

You can clear your saved situation in You. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to your operating system’s backup and restore settings. We cannot retrieve or delete notes held only on your device.

Optional searches and external links

When you choose Look up current topics, the entered interest is included in a Google news-search URL. Your preparation notes are not included. Google and your browser process the visit according to their own policies. Do not put private personal information into a search term.

Reference and support links also open external services. Apple may process App Store, payment, TestFlight or diagnostic information under its own terms and your settings. HelloGently does not receive your payment-card details through the app.

Contacting support

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments are processed to respond to your request. The support mailbox is hosted by Proton Mail. Proton states that its servers store data in Switzerland, Germany or Norway. See Proton Mail’s Privacy Policy for its processing practices. Your own email provider also processes messages you send. Do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

Resolved support emails and their attachments are deleted within 12 months after resolution, unless needed for an ongoing issue or a legal obligation. Support correspondence is used to respond to and manage your request, not for advertising.

This support website

The supplied pages do not include analytics scripts, advertising, forms, cookies or local storage. The selected hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP addresses and access logs to deliver and protect the site.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including visits without a GitHub account. GitHub may process data in the United States and other countries as described in its Privacy Statement, including its international-transfer safeguards. See also GitHub Pages information. No sign-in is required to read the published pages.

Your requests

You may contact the publisher to ask about personal information they hold about you or request correction or deletion, subject to applicable law. You can also contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner. Device-local app notes must be managed on your device.

Changes

This information will be updated if the app’s data practices change. The published version will show its effective date.
