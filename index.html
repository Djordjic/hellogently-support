A little help with HelloGently.

Questions about the app, feedback or a problem? Contact us by email.

hellogently.support@proton.me

For a technical issue, include your device model, iOS version, app version and the steps that led to the problem. Please leave private preparation notes and sensitive personal details out of screenshots.

Common questions

Does the app work offline?

The native mobile app includes its guides and practice content. External news searches, reference pages and email need an internet connection.

Where are my notes saved?

Your latest situation, preparation notes and preferences are stored on the device. They do not sync between devices. Use the same device to resume your plan.

How do I clear my saved situation?

Open You and choose Clear saved situation. You can also change the larger-text setting there. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to device backup and restore behavior.

Does HelloGently check current news?

No. The optional topic-search link opens Google news results for the interest you enter. Read the source and date before using a story as a conversation topic.

Do I need an account?

The native app does not require a HelloGently account. The separate web prototype may have a hosting-platform sign-in.

Is this medical support?

HelloGently provides practical suggestions for social situations. It is not a medical service, therapy or an emergency contact.
